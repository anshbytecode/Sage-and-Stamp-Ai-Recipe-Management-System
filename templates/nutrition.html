🌿 Ayurvedic Dosha Harmony Guide

Ayurveda categorizes foods based on elemental properties to promote metabolic equilibrium (Agni):

Vata Balancing (Air & Ether)

Favors warm, nourishing, unctuous gravies cooked in clarified ghee or sesame oil with warming spices (cumin, ginger, asafoetida).
Examples: Dal Makhani, Khichdi, Warm Rajma Chawal.

Pitta Balancing (Fire & Water)

Favors sweet, bitter, and astringent foods with cooling herbs (fresh coriander, mint, fennel, coconut, ghee) and moderate chili.
Examples: Palak Paneer, Masala Dosa with Coconut Chutney.

Kapha Balancing (Earth & Water)

Favors light, dry, stimulating, and warming preparations with black pepper, mustard seeds, turmeric, and crisp steamed textures.
Examples: Khaman Dhokla, Yellow Lentil Soup, Poha.
