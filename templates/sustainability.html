🌍 Why Cultural Compliance Protects the Planet

Eliminating beef and intensive red meats reduces the culinary carbon footprint by up to 74%. The traditional Indian dietary pyramid—grounded in pulses (lentils, chickpeas, beans), whole grains (basmati rice, millets, wheat), vegetables, and dairy (paneer, curd)—offers one of the world's most ecologically sustainable models for complete human nutrition.
