👨‍🍳 Smart Meal Prep Pro-Tips

  • Universal Onion-Tomato Base: Saute 1 kg onions and 1.5 kg tomatoes with ginger-garlic paste and turmeric. Store in clean glass jars for up to 5 days; use as a 5-minute base for Paneer, Chole, or Mushroom curries.
  • Legume Soaking Protocol: Always soak rajma and black dal in warm water with a pinch of baking soda for 12 hours. Cuts boiling energy costs by 50% and removes oligosaccharides.
  • Paneer Longevity: Store opened paneer submerged in a container of fresh cold water in the fridge, changing water daily. Stays fresh and tender for up to 6 days.
